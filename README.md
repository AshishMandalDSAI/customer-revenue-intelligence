## 🌟 What is NovaMart Customer 360?

**NovaMart Customer 360** is an end-to-end **Customer Revenue Intelligence Platform (CRIP)** designed to transform customer, transaction, and behavioral data into actionable business insights.

The platform combines customer analytics, machine learning, financial analysis, segmentation, recommendations, and interactive business intelligence into one integrated solution.

### 🚀 Platform Capabilities

* 🎯 Customer 360 Analytics
* 🔥 Churn Prediction
* 💰 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* ⚠️ Revenue-at-Risk Analysis
* 📊 Customer Profitability
* 🧩 RFM Analysis & Segmentation
* 🤖 Next-Best-Action Recommendations
* 🧠 AI Analytics Copilot
* 🚀 FastAPI REST Backend
* 📈 Interactive Streamlit Dashboard
* 📊 Power BI-Ready Analytics
* 🐘 PostgreSQL Database Design
* 🐳 Docker & Docker Compose
* 🧪 Automated Pytest Validation

The project uses a **synthetic e-commerce dataset** created specifically for academic, learning, and portfolio demonstration.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** NovaMart is a fictional company and the dataset is synthetic. Results are intended for academic and project demonstration only and should not be interpreted as real business performance.

# 🎯 Business Problem

Modern e-commerce businesses generate large volumes of customer, order, payment, product, campaign, and interaction data.

The challenge is not simply collecting this data.

The real challenge is turning this data into actionable business decisions.

NovaMart Customer 360 focuses on answering:

> **Which customers are at risk, which customers are valuable, how much revenue is at risk, which customers are profitable, and what action should the business take next?**

# ✨ Core Features

## 🎯 1. Customer 360 Analytics

Customer 360 provides a consolidated analytical view of customer behavior and value.

### Includes

* Customer profile
* Purchase behavior
* Transaction history
* Revenue contribution
* Customer value
* Churn risk
* Profitability
* RFM metrics
* Customer segment
* Recommended action

The objective is to move from isolated transaction records to a unified customer-level view.

---

# 🔥 2. Churn Prediction

The platform uses machine learning to identify customers who may be at risk of becoming inactive.

### Example Predictive Features

* Recency
* Frequency
* Monetary value
* Purchase frequency
* Average order value
* Customer tenure
* Discount behavior
* Engagement indicators
* Historical purchasing behavior

### Prediction Pipeline

```text
Customer Data
      ↓
Feature Engineering
      ↓
Churn ML Model
      ↓
Churn Probability
      ↓
Risk Classification
      ↓
Retention Recommendation
```

---

# 💰 3. Customer Lifetime Value

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** estimates the economic value associated with a customer over their relationship with the business.

### CLV Analysis Includes

* Historical revenue
* Purchase frequency
* Average order value
* Customer tenure
* Future value estimation
* Customer profitability

CLV helps the platform distinguish between customers based on their potential economic contribution.

---

# ⚠️ 4. Revenue-at-Risk

Revenue-at-Risk combines customer economic value with churn risk.

Instead of asking only:

> **Who might churn?**

the platform also asks:

> **How much revenue could potentially be affected by customer churn risk?**

### Conceptual Framework

```text
Customer Value
       ×
Churn Risk
       ↓
Revenue-at-Risk
```

This creates a financial perspective around customer retention analytics.

---

# 📊 5. Customer Profitability

Revenue alone does not necessarily indicate profitability.

NovaMart analyzes customer-level financial contribution using metrics such as:

* Revenue
* Cost
* Profit
* Profit margin
* Discount impact
* Customer-level contribution

This allows the platform to analyze both:

**Revenue Value**

and

**Profitability Value**

---

# 🧩 6. RFM Analysis & Customer Segmentation

RFM analysis evaluates customers using three core dimensions:

### Recency

How recently did the customer purchase?

### Frequency

How frequently does the customer purchase?

### Monetary

How much does the customer spend?

### Example Customer Segments

| Segment             | Typical Characteristics      |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Champions           | Recent, frequent, high-value |
| Loyal Customers     | Consistent purchasing        |
| Potential Loyalists | Strong engagement potential  |
| At Risk             | Declining activity           |
| Hibernating         | Low recent activity          |
| New Customers       | Recently acquired            |

The actual customer segments depend on the analytical pipeline and generated dataset.

Potential actions include:

Next-Best-Action
